Optimization, Contact, and Control from Scratch

Twenty-two slide decks that start at “what is a vector?” and end at reading contact-rich robotics papers, such as Hou & Mason's hybrid force-velocity control (ICRA 2019, ICRA 2021), without a walkthrough. Written for a strong high-school senior or a first-year undergraduate. Every tool is built before it is used, every number on a slide is recomputed in a companion notebook, and several slides run the real MuJoCo physics engine in the browser.

Status, September 2026: decks 11–16 are written; the other sixteen and the lab are planned (plan, contributor guide). Planned decks are listed in gray without links.

The decks

Part I. Mathematical toolkit

01. Vectors, matrices, and linear maps
Span, basis, matrices as maps, row reduction, rank, null space, solution sets. (planned)
02. Eigenvalues, singular values, and least squares
Projection, orthonormal bases, quadratic forms, SVD, condition number, least squares, minimum-norm solutions, the Schur complement. (planned)
03. Derivatives, gradients, and Jacobians
Taylor models, gradients and level sets, the Hessian, Jacobians, the chain rule, linearized dynamics, finite differences. (planned)
04. Probability, Gaussians, and sampling
Mean and variance, Gaussians and covariance, Monte Carlo, softmax weights, fitting elite samples. (planned)

Part II. Optimization

05. Unconstrained optimization: gradient descent and Newton's method
Optimality conditions, line search, convergence rates, conditioning, Newton, Gauss-Newton, projected gradient descent on the unit sphere. (planned)
06. Constrained optimization: Lagrange multipliers and the KKT conditions
Multipliers as prices, complementary slackness, KKT systems, penalty, augmented Lagrangian, barrier and projection methods. (planned)
07. Convexity, duality, and complementarity
Convex sets and functions, LP geometry and feasibility, cones, duality, LCP and NCP. (planned)

Part III. Trajectory optimization and control

08. Optimal control, dynamic programming, and LQR
Discrete-time optimal control, shooting and collocation, the Bellman equation, the Riccati recursion, MPC. (planned)
09. Differential dynamic programming and iLQR
The Q-function, backward and forward passes, regularization, control limits. (planned)
10. Sampling-based model predictive control and MuJoCo MPC
Predictive sampling, MPPI, CEM, splines, MuJoCo MPC's residuals and planners. (planned)

Part IV. Contact statics and grasping

11. Forces and Coulomb friction
Vectors, forces, the normal force, Coulomb's law, the 2D friction cone, the tilting-board experiment. [slides] [read]
12. The friction cone in 3D
The round cone and its slices, maximum dissipation, elliptic cones, pyramid approximations and what they cost, torsional friction, the dual cone. [slides] [read]
13. Multiple contacts and the contact wrench cone
Torque, slide-or-tip, center of pressure, the four-legged table, the contact wrench cone. [slides] [read]
14. Planar force closure with two fingers
Force closure, positive spans, Nguyen's theorem with a proof, when the normal-angle test is exact. [slides] [read]
15. Grasps in 3D
6D wrenches, soft fingers, antipodal pairs, three-finger grasps, the three-force theorem. [slides] [read]
16. Grasp quality metrics
Force closure as a convex hull or a linear program, the Ferrari–Canny quality, false positives from pyramids. [slides] [read]

Part V. Contact dynamics and simulation

17. Rigid-body motion: rotations, quaternions, twists, and wrenches
Configuration space, SO(3), gimbal lock, quaternions, why q-dot is not a velocity, twists, the adjoint, virtual work. (planned)
18. Rigid-body dynamics with contact
The manipulator equation, holonomic constraints, contact Jacobians, Signorini, impulses, time stepping, the quasi-static limit. (planned)
19. Contact solvers: LCP, CCP, and MuJoCo's soft contact
Pyramid LCPs and PGS, the Anitescu relaxation, solref and solimp in real MuJoCo, Drake, ADMM. (planned)
20. Complementarity-free contact
Closed-form forces as a spring in the dual cone, automatic Coulomb satisfaction, smoothing. (planned)

Part VI. Synthesis

21. Hybrid force-velocity control
Natural and artificial constraints, selection matrices, Hou & Mason 2019 and 2021, block tilting in MuJoCo. (planned)
22. Trajectory optimization through contact
Contact-implicit optimization, smoothing, sampling MPC through contact, reading a contact-rich paper. (planned)

Reading path for Hou & Mason
