Friction & Grasping from Scratch

Six short slide decks on Coulomb friction, friction cones, and force closure, starting from “what is a vector?” and ending at grasp quality metrics. Written for a strong high schooler or a first-year undergraduate; everything else is built along the way.

The decks

1. Pushing on Things
Vectors, forces, the normal force, Coulomb's law, and the 2D friction cone. The tilting-board experiment. [slides] [read]
2. The Friction Cone in 3D
The round cone and its slices, maximum dissipation, elliptic (anisotropic) cones, pyramid approximations and exactly what they cost, torsional friction, the dual cone. [slides] [read]
3. Many Contacts
Torque, slide-or-tip, center of pressure, the wobbly four-legged table, and the contact wrench cone. [slides] [read]
4. Holding Things with Two Fingers
Force closure, positive spans, squeezing, Nguyen's theorem with a proof, three worked examples, and when the normal-angle shortcut is exact. [slides] [read]
5. Grasping in 3D
Cross products and 6D wrenches, why two hard fingers never suffice, soft fingers, antipodal pairs (Chen & Burdick), three-finger grasps and the three-force theorem, Mirtich & Canny's optimum grasps. [slides] [read]
6. How Good Is a Grasp?
Force closure as a convex hull or a linear program, the Ferrari–Canny quality, and how pyramid approximations create false positives. [slides] [read]

PDF, PNG, PowerPoint

cd tutorial/slides
uv run export.py                 # every deck -> out/pdf/
uv run export.py --png --pptx    # also per-slide PNGs and an image-only .pptx

Or open a deck in Chrome and print it; the page size is already 1280×720. The HTML files are the source; the exports are throwaway copies.

Companion code

Every number on the slides is recomputed in two marimo notebooks: 

uvx marimo edit --sandbox tutorial/04_friction_cones.py   # parts 1-3
uvx marimo edit --sandbox tutorial/06_force_closure.py    # parts 4-6
